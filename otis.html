OTIS requirements before requesting the vertical measurement
  1. Pit depth, shaft width, shaft depth and the cutouts for the E&I panel and LOPs as per the approved OTIS BIM drawings.
  2. Centre line and grid lines clearly marked at the top and bottom of the shaft.
  3. Pit clean; extra rods and steel inside the shaft cut; unnecessary openings closed.
  4. Scaffolding with a working platform every 2 m from the starting level, with a ladder, to the OTIS required dimensions. OTIS to issue the scaffolding layout within 2 working days.
  5. Work permit issued by MBL before the plumbing work starts.
  6. Once a shaft is ready, inform the OTIS managers to plan the measurement.
